Know where your data goes.

The MCP server reads your authorized records and sends requested results to the assistant you connect. It does not persist health measurements; OAuth credentials are stored encrypted. Your assistant's own data policies also apply.

Coverage depends on your device, permissions and recorded data. Personal baselines describe your history and are not clinical reference ranges.
